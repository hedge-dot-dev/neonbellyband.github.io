Back at The Pond, Packed Again

Posted October 9, 2026 by Neon Belly

We could not be happier to be back at The Pond. It is where Neon Belly played its very first show in July, so getting invited back for October 3 felt like coming home.

And we packed the house again. Wall to wall, the whole night. The Pond said it better than we can on their own Instagram afterward: "Neon Belly tore it up, the bar was packed wall to wall." We will happily take that.

We also added 13 new songs to our set list, so there is a lot more to hear from us now than the last time you caught a show.

Thank you to The Pond for having us back, and to everyone who came out, sang along, and stuck around for the last song.

What is next: we are looking for the final shows to round out 2026, and we are starting to build our 2027 calendar. If you run a bar, a club, or a private event, or you know someone who does, now is the time to get us on the schedule.

And if you want to help, follow us on all of our socials and help us get the word of Neon Belly out there. Share a post, tell a friend, tag your favorite venue. It all helps.
