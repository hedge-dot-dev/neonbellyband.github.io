Kimbro's, Front to Back

Posted August 9, 2026 by Neon Belly

Some rooms you walk into and immediately want to earn. Kimbro's is one of those rooms, and for Hedge it was more than that: playing that stage has been a specific, standing dream of his for years, long before Neon Belly existed to make it happen.

If you have spent any time in Franklin you already know the Pickin' Parlor. It sits two blocks off Main in the old Antique District, part former neighborhood store, part Victorian cottage, open since 2005. Bluegrass one night, honky tonk the next, blues after that. A lot of music has moved through there. Getting our name on that calendar meant something to us before we ever plugged in.

Then the room filled up. All the way to the back, and it stayed that way.

We ran 90s and 2000s rock all night and the crowd carried more of it than we expected. There is a specific moment when a room stops being people who happened to be out and turns into people who are here for this, and we felt it land somewhere in the second set. Everything after that was easy.

Thanks to the crew at Kimbro's for looking after us, and to everyone who stuck around to the last song and asked for one more.
