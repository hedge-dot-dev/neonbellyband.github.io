Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Neon Belly is a cover band from Franklin, Tennessee. This page explains what information this website and the band's own posting tools use, and what they don't.

This Website

neonbellyband.com uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects things like your browser type, device, approximate location and the pages you visit. We don't collect your name or contact details unless you choose to email us. You can block analytics cookies in your browser or with Google's opt-out add-on.

Neon Belly Scheduler

Neon Belly Scheduler is a private tool the band uses to post our own performance videos to our own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. Only band members use it. It does not access anyone else's account.

When we connect our YouTube channel, the scheduler uses YouTube API Services to upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and read the status of videos on our channel. It stores the sign-in token only on the band's own computer and uses it only for those tasks. It does not collect, sell or share information about viewers or anyone else, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising.

By using YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The band can remove the scheduler's access to our channel at any time from the Google security settings page.
