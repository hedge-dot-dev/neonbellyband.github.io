Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · Disclosures

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Neon Belly is a cover band from Franklin, Tennessee. This policy explains what information neonbellyband.com and the band's own posting tools collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

The Short Version

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no forms, and no ads on this site.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits. That uses cookies.
  • Some pages load YouTube or Instagram content, which those companies handle under their own policies.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Your theme and language choices are saved on your own device only.

Information We Collect

Automatically. When you visit neonbellyband.com, Google Analytics collects things like your browser and device type, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), the pages you view, how long you stay, and how you arrived at the site. We use this only to understand which pages people read so we can improve the site.

When you contact us. If you email neonbellytn@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply, to talk about bookings, and to keep a record of the conversation.

When you tip or pay us. Tips and any merch payments go through Venmo. Venmo handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We see the details Venmo shows us, such as your name or username, the amount and any note you add.

On social media. If you follow, comment on, message or tag us on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, those platforms and we both see that activity, and the platforms' own policies apply.

Cookies and Device Storage

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell visits and visitors apart. Embedded videos (see below) may set their own cookies.

The site also saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage so they stick between visits: the color theme and fonts you pick with the record-sleeve switcher, and whether you chose Spanish. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services, Links and Embeds

This site uses or links to services run by other companies. They may collect information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads their content, and their own terms and privacy policies apply to that.

  • Google Analytics (visit counts). Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube (videos on the EPK and blog pages, and video thumbnails). On the EPK page the video player itself only loads after you click play. Blog posts load the player when the page opens. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram (an embedded video in one blog post). Instagram Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram links take you off this site. The "Share on Facebook" links only open Facebook's own share page when you click them.
  • Venmo (tips and merch payments). Venmo Privacy Statement.

Our fonts and images are hosted on this site, not fetched from a font service.

How We Use and Share Information

We use information to run and improve this website, answer your messages, arrange shows and bookings, and keep our own records. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers described above who help us run the site, or when the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account. We keep emails for as long as they are useful for bookings and band business, then delete them. You can ask us to delete an email thread sooner (see below).

Your Choices and Rights

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Clear the site's saved preferences by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.
  • Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, and visitors from the EU, UK and similar regions, may have additional rights over their personal information, including access, correction, deletion, and the right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. To use any right, email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

This site is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it. Many of our shows are at 21 and over venues.

Security

We keep the amount of personal information we hold small, and the site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Neon Belly Scheduler

Neon Belly Scheduler is a private tool the band uses to post our own performance videos to our own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. Only band members use it. It does not access anyone else's account.

When we connect our YouTube channel, the scheduler uses YouTube API Services to upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and read the status of videos on our channel. It stores the sign-in token only on the band's own computer and uses it only for those tasks. It does not collect, sell or share information about viewers or anyone else, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising.

By using YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The band can remove the scheduler's access to our channel at any time from the Google security settings page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions
